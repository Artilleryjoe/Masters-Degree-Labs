# Master of Science in Cybersecurity – Lab Portfolio

Maintained by **Kristopher McCoy**  
Veteran • Practitioner • Focused on secure systems, ethical hacking, and defensive strategy

---

## Overview
This repository is a course-by-course collection of hands-on labs, reports, and research completed throughout the Master of Science in Cybersecurity program. Every folder corresponds to a class and contains self-contained exercises or writeups designed for professional review. All activities were performed in controlled or ethical environments (e.g., virtual labs, DVWA, Mutillidae) and adhere to academic, legal, and responsible disclosure standards.
